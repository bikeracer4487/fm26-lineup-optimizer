# Mentoring fixtures

`arrow-readings-2026-11-14.json` holds the influence arrows the game showed for 24 mentoring groups of the owner's first team in Football Manager 27, read by the owner on 14 November 2026 and saved here with his agreement.

How they were taken: in the game's Mentoring screen a group is three players with an arrow each way between every pair, in one of four strengths (none, light, average, significant). The owner set each group, read its six arrows, and reported every arrow above none. The first four groups are the ones the game made itself, the next four are the ones this app suggested at the time, and the rest were chosen to test particular questions. The pairs under `confirmedOnReReading` were surprising and were read a second time.

Players are named as in the captures under `../captures/`. The readings belong to the squad as it stood on 14 November 2026.
