# FM27 Advisors Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** The six advisors of design section 8.7, as engine code the app can call: the First and Second XI with squad tiers, Depth, Rest, Training (position retraining), Player Removal with the loan review, and a suggested importance for each fixture.

**Architecture:** A new `Advisors/` directory in the `LineupEngine` package. Each advisor is a pure function from the squad, a tactic (or the saved tactics) and the engine's parameters to a report made of plain values, with a plain-words reason on every piece of advice. They share one calculation of the squad's elevens (`SquadTiers`, built in the planner plan). Nothing reads files; `lineup-cli` gains an `advise` command for trying them on the real captures. What the owner accepts or rejects, and familiarity recorded at each import, is state the app keeps and hands in.

**Tech Stack:** Swift 6, SwiftPM, Swift Testing, Foundation only. Same package and conventions as the engine and planner plans.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-12-fm27-native-app-design.md`, sections 8.7, 8.8, 10 and 11. Read 8.7 before any task.

## Global Constraints

- No silent defaults. A bad or missing value is an error that names what is wrong. An attribute an advisor's rule needs and a player lacks is `RatingError.missingAttribute`, naming the player and the attribute.
- Every tunable number lives in `EngineParameters`, with an evidence label and a source. Numbers that share one evidence label and one source may be grouped in a small struct held by one `Parameter`. In this plan [FM27 obs] is `.fm27Observation`, [FM26] is `.carriedOverFM26` and [derived] is `.derived`. "branch" means the old Python on branch customizable-tactics; research files are cited as `docs/Research/<file>`.
- Prefer FM27 evidence. Where FM27 research is silent, use the FM26 research and label it as carried over (owner's rule, spec 8.4).
- Every piece of advice carries `basis: Evidence`: the weakest evidence among the parameters that produced it, so the interface can mark advice that rests on carried-over or derived numbers (spec 8.8). Weakest to strongest: derived, carried over from FM26, FM27 observation, FM27 official, FM27 measured.
- Every rule gets a test where it changes an outcome and a test where it does not.
- The same inputs always give the same report, whatever the order of the input arrays: loops run over players by `PlayerID.key`, slots in `SlotID` order, tactics in the order given.
- Every `switch` over an engine error, warning, cause, level or action is exhaustive, with no `default`.
- Reasons are plain sentences a manager can read. Percentages round down as `Display.percent` does. Money is whole pounds with thousands separators: "£1,250 a week".
- Foundation only. No build warnings. `swift test` passes after every task, including the 594-case FM26 reference check, unchanged.
- Do not modify Python, `ui/`, `.bat` files, existing fixtures, `Resources/rating_tables.json` or `Resources/rating_profile_fm27.json`.
- The repository is public: no player data outside `LineupEngine/Tests/LineupEngineTests/Fixtures/`.
- Paths in tasks are relative to `LineupEngine/` unless they start with `docs/` or are `CLAUDE.md`.
- Old faults that must not come back (spec 11, and the review of the old advisors): tiers from a formation that does not match the tactic; money and wage rules that depend on the unit; one rejection per player; an estimate that claims to use Versatility and does not; code that is computed and never shown.

## Review Focus

Inputs the spec implies but does not spell out, most likely first. Each has a test in the task named.

1. **A slot nobody can cover, or a squad smaller than eleven.** Expected: the elevens leave it unfilled, Depth shows it with no options and as thin, and Training reports it as a need with no candidate. No crash. (Tasks 1, 2, 4)
2. **Two slots that share a position and only two players for them** (two centre-backs for D_CL and D_CR). Expected: both slots are thin: a first choice elsewhere is not cover. (Task 2)
3. **Numbers at the edge of a division**: current ability 0, potential below current, age 24 or more in the growth-rate rule, every wage 0, months left unknown. Expected: the rule concerned does not apply, or the cost is absent; never a crash or a not-a-number. (Task 5)
4. **A fixture with no other fixtures and no history.** Expected: the schedule changes nothing and the suggestion comes from the competition and opponent alone, or from the unknown base of 50. (Task 6)
5. **Retraining observations that go backwards or are missing**: familiarity falls between imports, or there is one observation, or none. Expected: "no data" or "stalled", never a negative number of weeks. (Task 4)

  ```swift
  public struct DepthOption: Equatable, Sendable {
      public let player: PlayerID
      public let name: String
      public let squad: Squad
      public let rating: Double               // full-fitness slot rating
      public let ipFamiliarity: Int
      public let oopFamiliarity: Int
      public let isSolid: Bool                // both familiarities at or above depthSolidFamiliarity
      public let unavailable: Exclusion?      // today, at medium importance; nil when he can play
      public let tier: SquadTier?
      public let alsoNeededAt: [SlotID]       // other slots where he is first or second choice
  }
  public struct SlotDepth: Equatable, Sendable {
      public let slot: SlotID
      public let ipPosition: Position
      public let oopPosition: Position
      public let options: [DepthOption]       // best first
      public let onLoan: [DepthOption]        // out on loan, shown apart
      public let cover: Int                   // see the rule
      public let isThin: Bool
      public let dropToSecond: Double?        // first choice's rating minus second choice's; nil without both
  }
  public struct DepthReport: Equatable, Sendable {
      public let slots: [SlotDepth]           // in slot order
      public let thin: [SlotID]               // most in need first
  }
  public enum Depth {
      public static func report(tactic: Tactic, players: [Player], ownClub: String?, catalog: RoleCatalog,
                                profile: RatingProfile, parameters: EngineParameters) throws -> DepthReport
  }
  ```

New parameters:

| Name | Value | Evidence and source |
|---|---|---|
| `depthCoverFamiliarity` | 10 | [derived] the Competent step of the familiarity table: below it a player is out of position |
| `depthSolidFamiliarity` | 15 | [derived] spec 8.7's starting rule; FM27 Match Lab Study 5 puts a cap of 15 at 0.26 points a game and a cap of 10 at 0.85 (`docs/Research/fm27-tactical-effectiveness-and-roles.md`) |
| `depthThinBelow` | 2 | [derived] spec 8.7's starting rule: fewer than two is thin |

Rules:

- **Options.** Every player not out on loan whose familiarity is at least `depthCoverFamiliarity` at the slot's in-possession position and at its out-of-possession position, ranked by full-fitness slot rating, then by key. Players out on loan who would qualify go in `onLoan`, ranked the same way.
- **Cover.** The number of options that are solid, available today (`unavailable == nil`, from `RiskRules.exclusion` at medium importance with `isNextFixture` true), and not first choice at another slot. A first choice elsewhere is already playing; he is not cover (Review Focus 2).
- **Thin.** `cover < depthThinBelow`.
- **Drop to second.** From the elevens' tiers: the first choice's rating in this slot minus the second choice's.
- **Shared cover.** `alsoNeededAt` lists the other slots where the option is first or second choice.
- **Where to recruit.** `thin` lists the thin slots: least cover first, then the larger drop to second (a slot with no second choice counts as the largest), then slot order.

New parameters:

| Name | Value | Evidence and source |
|---|---|---|
| `restLimitRules` | age 32 or over: base 300; age 30 or 31: 350; under 19: 350; otherwise the existing `jadedThreshold`. Then Natural Fitness below 10: −50, 15 or over: +50; Stamina below 10: −50, 15 or over: +30; Injury Proneness 15 or over: −100, 8 or under: +50. Kept within 200 to 550 | [FM26] branch `fm_match_ready_selector.py` `_get_adjusted_fatigue_threshold`; no FM27 evidence beyond the 400 line |
| `restWatchShare` | 0.8 | [FM26] branch `ui/api/api_rest_advisor.py` (warning at 80% of the limit) |
| `holidayMargin` | 200 | [derived] `docs/Research/physical-condition-claude.md`: warnings at 400 to 500, the rest icon near 600 |
| `holidayJadednessRecoveryPerDay` | 50 | [FM26] `docs/Research/jadedness-fatigue.md`: rest sessions with a holiday recovered 152 in three days |

Rules, for every player who is not out on loan and not injured:

- **His limit** comes from `restLimitRules`. `limitNotes` says each adjustment that applied, for example "aged 32 or over", "Natural Fitness below 10".
- **His run of starts** counts confirmed lineups dated on or before `asOf`.
- **Level**, the first that applies:
  - holiday: jadedness above `limit + holidayMargin`;
  - rest: jadedness above `limit`, or condition below `conditionFloor`;
  - watch: jadedness above `restWatchShare × limit`, or his run of starts carries a factor under the planner's consecutive-starts rule (looked up exactly as the planner does at medium importance).
  - Otherwise he is not listed.
- **Days.** To get back under the watch line: for a holiday, `(jadedness − watch line) / holidayJadednessRecoveryPerDay`; for rest, the same over `jadednessRecoveryPerDay`; both rounded up. When condition is the cause, the number of rest days `FitnessProjection.afterDays` needs at `intensity` to bring him to the floor (at most 14, else nil). When both apply, the larger. nil for watch.
- **Reasons**, one sentence per cause, in this order and wording:
  - "Jadedness 640 is more than 200 above his limit of 400: a holiday clears it fastest"
  - "Jadedness 450 is above his limit of 400: rest him from training and matches"
  - "Condition 78% is below the 85% floor"
  - "Jadedness 330 is close to his limit of 400"
  - "Has started the last 4 matches in quick succession"
- **Basis.** The weakest evidence among: `jadedThreshold` when his limit is the standard one, otherwise `restLimitRules`; `holidayMargin` for a holiday; `conditionFloor` when condition is a cause; `restWatchShare` and the consecutive-starts parameters for watch.
- **Order.** Holiday, rest, watch; then jadedness, highest first; then key.
- `FitnessModel.problems(in:)` also reports `restWatchShare` outside 0 to 1, and a recovery rate that is not above 0.

**Interfaces:**

- Produces:
  ```swift
  public struct RetrainingChoice: Hashable, Codable, Sendable {
      public let player: PlayerID
      public let position: Position
  }
  public struct RetrainingNeed: Equatable, Sendable { public let tactic: String; public let slot: SlotID }
  public enum RetrainingDifficulty: String, CaseIterable, Sendable { case fluid, structural, spatial, inversion }
  public struct RetrainingRecommendation: Equatable, Sendable {
      public let choice: RetrainingChoice
      public let name: String
      public let age: Int
      public let currentFamiliarity: Int
      public let needs: [RetrainingNeed]          // the thin slots he would cover
      public let ratingIfLearnt: Double           // his rating in the first of those slots at familiarity 20 in both phases
      public let shareOfFirstChoice: Double       // that rating over the slot's first choice
      public let learnability: Double             // 0...1
      public let from: Position                   // the position he retrains from
      public let difficulty: RetrainingDifficulty
      public let weeks: Int                       // estimate to reach depthSolidFamiliarity
      public let reasons: [String]
      public let cautions: [String]
      public let basis: Evidence                  // .carriedOverFM26

      public let recommendations: [RetrainingRecommendation]   // most needed first
      public let needsWithoutCandidates: [RetrainingNeed]       // thin slots nobody suits: recruit

  public enum Training {
      public static func report(tactics: [Tactic], players: [Player], ownClub: String?,
                                accepted: [Retraining], rejected: Set<RetrainingChoice>,
                                catalog: RoleCatalog, profile: RatingProfile,
                                parameters: EngineParameters) throws -> TrainingReport
  }

  public struct FamiliarityObservation: Equatable, Codable, Sendable { public let day: Day; public let familiarity: Int }
  public struct RetrainingProgress: Equatable, Sendable {
      public enum State: String, Sendable { case noData, improving, stalled, reached }
      public let state: State
      public let start: Int?
      public let latest: Int?
      public let weeks: Double                // from the first observation to the last
      public let gainedPerWeek: Double?       // nil with under one week of observations
      public let weeksToSolid: Int?           // nil unless improving
  }
  public enum RetrainingTracker {
      public static func progress(of retraining: Retraining, observations: [FamiliarityObservation],
  ```

New parameters:

| Name | Value | Evidence and source |
|---|---|---|
| `learnabilityRules` | weights: Versatility 0.45, age 0.25, Professionalism 0.20, room to grow 0.10. Versatility counts as `Versatility / 20`, times 0.3 below 10. Age factor: under 21: 1.0; 21 to 23: 0.95; 24 or 25: 0.70; 26 or 27: 0.40; 28 or 29: 0.15; 30 or over: 0.05. Professionalism counts as `Professionalism / 20`. Room to grow is `(potential − current) / 30`, kept within 0 to 1 | [FM26] branch `fm_training_advisor.py` (training score and age factor) |
| `retrainingMinimumLearnability` | 0.40 | [derived] the branch asked for a score above 0.6 or an age under 24; one line between them |
| `retrainingMinimumShare` | 0.80 | [FM26] `docs/Research/lineup-depth-strategy.md` and the rotation guide: cover can be about 20% worse than the first choice |
| `retrainingPerPosition` | 2 | [FM26] branch `fm_training_advisor.py` (quotas of one or two per position) |
| `retrainingTransitions` | the branch's table of pairs to classes, verbatim (35 pairs), with its default: both positions among GK, D, WB and DM, or both among AM and ST: structural; otherwise spatial | [FM26] branch `scoring_parameters.py` `POSITION_TRANSITIONS` and `get_retraining_difficulty` |
| `retrainingWeeks` | fluid 4 to 8; structural 12 to 20; spatial 24 to 36; inversion: not recommended | [FM26] branch `scoring_parameters.py` `RETRAINING_DIFFICULTY` |
| `retrainingPace` | age: 21 or under ×1.0; 22 to 26 ×1.3; 27 to 31 ×1.6; 32 or over ×1.9. Versatility 15 or over ×0.6; 9 or under ×1.5; otherwise ×1.0. Head start: familiarity 13 or over ×0.5; 10 to 12 ×0.7 | [FM26] branch `ui/api/api_training_advisor.py` (age plasticity 1.0, 0.7, 0.4, 0.1 as `2 − plasticity`; head start) and `docs/Research/position-retraining-strategy.md` (three to six months with high Versatility, 18 months or never with low) |
| `retrainingStalledAfterWeeks` | 6 | [derived] this design's choice |

The branch's positions "ST" map to `ST (C)`; "D(L)" to `D (L)` and so on.

Rules:

- **Needs.** For each saved tactic, `Depth.report`; every thin slot is a need. Needs are taken tactic by tactic in the order given, slots in slot order.
- **Candidates for a need** are players who are the club's own (not loaned in or out), not goalkeepers for an outfield slot or outfield players for the goalkeeper's (a goalkeeper is a player whose familiarity at GK is 15 or over), and who:
  - are not already solid in the slot (a familiarity below `depthSolidFamiliarity` at the slot's in-possession or out-of-possession position);
  - are not first choice in another thin slot of the same tactic (he is needed where he is);
  - would rate at least `retrainingMinimumShare` of the slot's first choice with familiarity 20 at both of the slot's positions (rate a copy of the player with those two familiarities raised);
  - have `learnability` of at least `retrainingMinimumLearnability`;
  - are not ruled out by an inversion.
- **The position to learn** is the slot's in-possession position if he is below solid there, otherwise its out-of-possession position.
- **From.** Among his positions at familiarity 18 or over, the one with the easiest class to the position to learn (ties: the lower `Position` raw value); with none at 18, his highest familiarity (same tie rule).
- **Weeks.** The middle of the class's range × the three `retrainingPace` factors, rounded up, at least 4.
- **Choosing.** A (player, position) pair in `rejected`, or one already in `accepted`, is skipped. Each player gets at most one recommendation: the one with the highest `shareOfFirstChoice × learnability`. Each position gets at most `retrainingPerPosition`, the highest of that product first. A pair that covers several needs lists them all in `needs`.
- **Order.** By the order of the first need each covers (thinner slots first within a tactic, as `DepthReport.thin` orders them), then by the product, highest first, then key.
- **A need no recommendation covers** goes in `needsWithoutCandidates` (Review Focus 1).
- **Reasons**, in this order:
  - "Cover at D (L) is thin in Sample 4-2-3-1 (1 solid player available)"
  - "He would rate 104.2 there once learnt, 92% of the first choice"
  - "From D (R): a fluid change, about 9 weeks at his age and Versatility"
- **Cautions**, each when it applies, in this order:
  - always: "Timings are estimates from earlier versions of the game; none is published for FM27"
  - always: "His familiarity at D (R) may fall while he learns a new position"
  - familiarity there under 10: "He may never become natural there"
  - Versatility under 10: "Low Versatility: this may take 18 months or more"
  - the position is the slot's out-of-possession position only: "Needed out of possession only: reports say out-of-possession familiarity was hard to train in FM26"
- **Progress.** Observations on or after `acceptedOn`, in day order (two on one day: the later in the array). None: `noData`. `latest` at or above `depthSolidFamiliarity`: `reached`. `gainedPerWeek = (latest − start) / weeks` once `weeks` is at least 1. Stalled: `weeks` at least `retrainingStalledAfterWeeks` and no gain. Improving otherwise, with `weeksToSolid = (depthSolidFamiliarity − latest) / gainedPerWeek` rounded up when the rate is above 0. Nothing is ever negative (Review Focus 5).

## Changes made while building

Decisions taken during the build that differ from, or add to, the tasks above. Task 8 holds the largest: the rules changed after the real squad was run.

- Every engine task also keeps the app building, since the app depends on the engine.
- `DepthReport` carries a `basis`, like every other piece of advice.
- A retraining recommendation's basis is the weakest evidence among the parameters behind it, which is "derived" with the standard parameters, not the fixed "carried over from FM26" of Task 4's interface.
- `TrainingReport` has a third list, `needsBeingRetrained`: a thin slot someone accepted is already learning is not listed as "recruit". It counts only when the accepted position is one the player is still short in.
- Retraining pairs are chosen best first (share of the first choice times learnability), one per player and two per position, so a player whose best position is full is offered his next best.
- Needs are numbered in tactic order, then thinnest slot first.
- The goalkeeper's slot never gets a retraining candidate: a goalkeeper is already solid in goal by definition, and outfield players are not offered it. A thin goalkeeper's slot always reads "recruit".
- The 14-day horizon of Rest's condition estimate is a parameter (`restConditionEstimateDays`).
- Parameter checks: bad rest, training, removal and depth numbers are reported by the same check the planner runs, so a bad advisor parameter also stops a plan.
- Task 8: cover counts players who are not injured rather than "available today"; the drop is to the best player who can step in (`dropToNextBest`); a retraining candidate is never a first choice elsewhere and must already be solid in the slot's other phase; Removal ranks "the weakest 30%" by share of a slot's first choice and takes 30 points off a player who is real cover where cover is thin or would become thin; the crowded-schedule window is the match day and the six days before it.
- After the whole-plan review: a player above the flat jadedness line the lineup uses is at least "rest" in Rest, whatever his own limit; Rest's basis also counts the numbers behind its day estimate; Removal's squad average and median wage are over the first team only; only "out of contract" is read as free to release, and a contract type the tool does not read gets no points and unknown costs; "a bigger match follows" applies only when the next match scores higher than this one (Task 6's league-before-league reduction is gone); `Training.report` takes the players Removal advises moving on (`RemovalReport.advisedToLeave`) and never recommends them, with the caller wiring the two; `ImportanceAdvisor.suggest` throws on bad parameters; `DepthOption.countsAsCover` and `SlotDepth.nextBest` let a screen mark cover without repeating the rule.

## Known limits, for the owner

- Depth counts cover by familiarity, not quality: a weak youth player at familiarity 15 counts. The drop to the next best player shows the quality gap beside it.
- "No next best" means nobody else could step in without leaving another first-eleven slot; it does not suggest moving a first choice across.
- The second eleven is the best eleven from the players the first did not use, so it can hold a player far out of position (an outfield player in goal). His familiarity is shown beside him.
- Rest advice does not look at the fixtures to come. It says who is over his limit or under the condition floor today, so a one-day dip in condition reads as "rest"; the planner decides who starts.
- Training recommends position retraining only. It cannot see what a player is being trained in: accepting a recommendation is how the app is told. Long changes from familiarity 1 are offered with their cautions.
- A retraining candidate's rating is compared with the slot's current first choice. Where that first choice is weak or out of position, almost anyone passes.
- Player Removal's protection for real cover lowers a score; it does not overrule one. A player can still be "terminate" while one reason says cover would be thin without him. With most slots at cover of two, many players carry that reason.
- In a small squad a sound backup can be "among the weakest 30%".
- Player Removal judges a player against one tactic (the primary). A player who is first choice in another saved tactic is not protected by that.
- The retraining timings, the limits for age and attributes in Rest, the importance tables and the whole of Player Removal are numbers from earlier versions of the game or from the old app, and are marked as such wherever they are shown. Retraining progress tracking exists so the owner can see real speeds in their own save.
- The importance suggestion knows the competition and the opponent only as the owner sets them. Without them it uses the schedule alone.
- Left out on purpose: the old app's extra removal rules by type of position, and its rule for attackers of 29 or over with low Natural Fitness. They were thresholds on hidden attributes with no source.
