# Offers: terms to propose when selling or loaning out a player. Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A new Offers screen gives, for each player marked to sell or to loan out, the terms to ask of clubs in general and of one named club, says what to answer to a bid, keeps a record of what clubs answered, and replaces three estimates with figures measured from that record.

**Architecture:** The engine gains one rules entry (`offerRules`), the record's types, three pure functions (`Offers.ask`, `Offers.proposal`, `Offers.measures`) and the words (`AdvisorText+Offers.swift`). Core saves the typed values, the clubs' figures, the asks and the deals in a file of its own (`offers.json`), and builds the screen's sheet from the marks, the squads and that file. The views add one screen and one editor sheet. Nothing plans and no other advice reads the file.

**Tech Stack:** Swift 6, macOS 26, SwiftUI and Observation, Swift Testing, Foundation only. Two packages: `LineupEngine/` and `LineupApp/` (targets `LineupAppCore`, `LineupAppUI`, `LineupApp`).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-22-fm27-offers-design.md`. Read it before any task; section numbers below are its. The research is `docs/Research/fm27-sale-and-loan-offer-acceptance.md`. The project's rules are in `CLAUDE.md`.

## Calls this plan makes beyond the design

Each is the owner's to change.

1. **A loan gets one counter too.** The design limits a sale to one counter. Without the same limit a loan's suggestion would repeat one counter for ever, so after a counter of the owner's is answered by terms of the club's, the advice is to accept or leave it.
2. **"Waiting for their answer."** While the owner's last round in a deal has no answer, the deal's suggestion is that one line.
3. **No command on the command line.** The captured squad has no values, so `lineup-cli` gets no `offer` command.
4. **The mark sets the kind.** A player marked to sell gets sale terms only, and one marked to loan out gets loan terms only.
5. **Money is written in full**, "£35,000", as the rest of the app writes it, not "£35K" as the design's sketch has it.
6. **A club's wage figures are saved by the week**, converted from the period FMRTE is set to when typed, as a player's wage is. A later change of FMRTE's period then shows them converted, not misread.
7. **What is left after instalments goes after appearances.** Instalments are 44% of the later money rounded to two significant figures; the appearances money is the remainder, so the parts always add up to the total.

## Review Focus

Conditions the design implies and a person will meet, each pinned by a test in the task named:

1. Value 0, wage 0 or budget 0: no division by zero, no "nan" or "inf" in any sentence, and the terms the design gives for 0 (Tasks 1, 3, 4 and 5).
2. A value retyped in the middle of a deal: earlier rounds keep the value they were made on, and each ratio uses its own round's value (Tasks 4 and 6).
3. A player whose mark is taken off, or who has left, while a deal is open: the deal is listed under "Earlier offers", still counts in the measures, and nothing crashes for a key no held player has (Task 7).
4. The same club typed with other capitals, spacing or accents: its figures fill the fields and its open deal is found, not a second one opened (Tasks 6 and 7).
5. `offers.json` on disk is not readable: the app starts with nothing recorded and a notice, keeps the file aside, and recording then works and saves (Task 6).

### Task 2: A sale's next step: accept, counter, step down

**Files:**
- Modify: `LineupEngine/Sources/LineupEngine/Advisors/Offers.swift`
- Test: `LineupEngine/Tests/LineupEngineTests/OffersSaleTests.swift`

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: Task 1's types; `Offers.ask`, `Offers.proposal`.
- Produces: `Offers.ask(... earlier:)` and `Offers.proposal(... rounds:)` for `.sale` with a history. The rules, from section 3 of the design:
  - **Last round the club's**, total b: b at or above T gives `.accept`. Otherwise, when the rounds hold a club round, then an owner round, before this one (the club has answered a counter), `.acceptOrLeave`.   - **Last round the owner's, no answer:** `.awaitAnswer`. Answer `.accepted`: the deal is agreed and Core does not ask; return `.accept`.
  - **Last round the owner's, refused or no reply:** the run is the trailing owner rounds so answered, back to the last club round. A run above `stepsDownAtMost` gives `.nothingLower(.clubTurnedDownThree)`. Otherwise the total is `rounded((1 − stepDown) × that round's total)`, at most T, and at least the highest total among the club's rounds; split as a first proposal is (so it needs the budget), with `.steppedDown` among the notes. A counter's sentence lumps the club's own later money with what is added ("£8,000 now and £3,600 later, after 50 league appearances"); that is accepted as a small fault.
  - **`ask` with earlier asks:** when the latest has `noBidCame`, the run is the trailing asks with it. A run above `stepsDownAtMost` gives `.nothingLower(.noBidThree)`; otherwise the total steps down from the latest ask's total, at most T, with `.steppedDown`. When the latest has not, the first ask's terms.
  - Rounds whose terms are not `.sale` throw `OfferError.wrongKind`.

- Produces: `Offers.ask` and `Offers.proposal` for `.loan`. The rules, from section 4 of the design, with W his wage a week, V his value, L `loanLinePerWeek` and τ = L × V:
  - **The share** is the smallest of 100, τ ÷ W, and for a club its wage left ÷ W and, when entered, its highest wage ÷ W, as a percentage rounded down to a multiple of `wageStepPercent` (absorb floating-point error before rounding down, as `Display.percent` does, so 150 ÷ 500 is 30). The note `.shareLimitedBy` names the limit that set it, the first of value, wage left, highest wage on a tie, and `.all` at 100.
  - **The fee a month** is asked only when the share is 100: `weeksPerMonthInRule × (bears − W)`, where `bears` is τ, and for a club the smaller of τ and its wage left; rounded by `rounded`; nothing when not above 0.
  - **A wage of 0:** the share is 0, no `.shareLimitedBy` note, and the fee rule applies with W = 0.
  - **The other terms:** development gives `playingTime: .startingPlace`, `recall: true`, `futureFee: nil`. Wage gives `playingTime: nil`, `recall: true`, `futureFee: nil` and the note `.optionalFutureFee(rounded(saleLine × V))` when that is above 0. A fee above 0 with the recall kept adds `.recallWithFee`. To clubs in general `.clubsOpenLow` is added. The loan's own notes are in this order: `.shareLimitedBy`, `.recallWithFee`, `.optionalFutureFee`, `.clubsOpenLow`.
  - A loan to a club needs `wageBudgetPerWeek` and `wagesSpentPerWeek` (`OfferError.missing(.wageBudget, club:)`, then `.wagesSpent`), for a first proposal, a step down, and the wage purpose's answer to the club's terms. A development loan's answer to the club's terms needs neither.
  - **Last round the club's**, by purpose. Development: its `playingTime` is `.startingPlace` gives `.accept`; otherwise `.counter` with its share and fee, `.startingPlace`, recall kept. Wage: its share at or above the suggested share gives `.accept`; otherwise `.counter` with the smaller of the suggested share and its share plus one step, its fee, no playing time. Either way, when the club has already answered a counter of the owner's (a club round, then an owner round, before this one), `.acceptOrLeave` (this plan's call 1).
  - **Last round the owner's:** no answer gives `.awaitAnswer`. Refused or no reply: from the terms he recorded there, drop the fee if it is above 0, else lower the share one step; never above the present suggestion's share, and no fee where the present suggestion has none; with `.steppedDown`. At a share of 0 with no fee and refused or unanswered: `.nothingLower(.clubDoesNotWantHim)`. In a deal there is no limit of two.
  - **`ask` with earlier asks:** as the sale's, with the loan's step: the latest with `noBidCame` steps down, a run above `stepsDownAtMost` gives `.nothingLower(.noBidThree)`, and at a share of 0 with no fee and no bid `.nothingLower(.noBidThree)` too.

### Task 4: What the offers have shown

**Files:**
- Create: `LineupEngine/Sources/LineupEngine/Advisors/OfferMeasures.swift`
- Modify: `LineupEngine/Sources/LineupEngine/Advisors/Offers.swift`
- Test: `LineupEngine/Tests/LineupEngineTests/OfferMeasuresTests.swift` (new)

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: Task 1's types.
- Produces:
  - `OfferTerms.ratio(value: Int, wagePerWeek: Double, rules: OfferRules) -> Double?`: a sale's `total ÷ value`; a loan's `(wagePercent ÷ 100 × wage + feePerMonth ÷ weeksPerMonthInRule) ÷ value`; nil when the value is 0.
  - `public static func Offers.measures(asks: [OfferAsk], deals: [OfferDeal], from: Day?, parameters: EngineParameters) -> OfferMeasures`. Section 6 of the design, exactly:
    - Only asks and rounds dated on or after `from` are read (all of them when it is nil). `measuredFrom` is `from`.
    - For each kind, per deal: **paid** is the highest ratio among the club's rounds and the owner's rounds answered `.accepted`; **turned down** is the lowest ratio among the owner's rounds answered `.refused` and the owner's rounds followed at once by a club's round of a lower ratio. A round with no ratio, and a round answered `.noReply`, count for neither.
    - An ask is turned down at its ratio when some deal for the same player and kind has a first round by the club, dated on or after the ask and before that player's next ask of the kind, with a lower ratio.
    - `deals` counts the deals of the kind with a round in the window, `agreed` those with an accepted round in it, `paid` the deals that gave one, `turnedDown` the deals and asks that gave one, `since` the earliest day among those rounds and asks.
    - `line` is set when `paid` and `turnedDown` are each at least `fewestEachWay` and `medianPaid` is below `medianTurnedDown`: midway between them. A median sorts the ratios by value and takes the mean of the middle two for an even count.
    - `counterStep`: over sale deals in which a round of the owner's follows the club's first round and a later round was accepted, the accepted round's total ÷ the club's first round's total (a first total of 0 is left out); `counterDeals` is how many; the median when there are at least `fewestCounterDeals`.
  - `Offers.ask` and `Offers.proposal` use `measures.sale.line` in place of `saleLine`, `measures.loan.line` in place of `loanLinePerWeek` and `measures.counterStep` in place of `counterStep` whenever each is not nil, and then append `.measuredLine` or `.measuredCounterStep` to the notes of a suggestion that used it. A measured counter step of 1 or less gives `.acceptOrLeave`.

## What changed during the build

- **An ask still waiting changes nothing.** The plan's "the first ask's terms" was read by two implementers as the terms of the first ask recorded; after Task 3's review the suggestion is the present terms from his present value, for sales and for loans, until "No bid came" is set.
- **An agreed deal says so.** A deal with any accepted round gets the advice `agreed`, "The deal is agreed." (`OfferAdvice.agreed`, added after Task 5's review), in place of telling the owner to accept what is already agreed.
- **Sentences that could be false were reworded** after Task 5's review. The share limits say "is within" ("40% is within what Barnet have left in their wage budget: £200 a week."), since the share is rounded down to a step and "is" contradicted the figure beside it. A measured figure is said as what the record shows ("The line your own offers show is 51% of his value (this tool's estimate).", "… is 2.1% of his value a month …" for a loan, "Your own agreed sales show clubs coming up 15% over a bid (this tool's estimate)."), since the first words claimed the terms shown were at the line, which is false for a lowered ask, a counter capped at the ask or a value of 0. The evidence note says "a counter of up to 20% over a bid". For these, `OfferNote.measuredLine` and `.measuredCounterStep` carry the figure used, and `ShareLimit.wageLeft` and `.highestWage` the club's figure a week. The loan rule's divisor is left out of a sentence when the rule's figure is 0 or not finite, so it cannot trap.
- **Nothing stays nothing.** A step down from an ask of nothing (a value of 0) stays at nothing, and "Three asks have drawn no bid" is said only after the run of asks passes two.
- **`OfferField` gained `wage`**, so a wage below 0 is refused by name.
- **A goalkeeper is read as the design says**: his pasted positions are "GK" and nothing else. The first build read it from familiarity in goal, which gave an outfield player solid in goal "First-Choice Goalkeeper"; it was corrected.
- **Recording says what became of it.** `AppModel.record(_ draft:)` (the plan called it `save`) returns `OfferRecordResult`: recorded, refused with the engine's sentence, or ignored (inside the half-second guard, or with a blank club), so the sheet closes only when something was recorded. The model opens the sheet from the player's mark and loan purpose (`offerDraft(for:club:purpose:filledWith:)`), so the club's offer for a development loan opens at less than a starting place.
- **Deals.** `openDeal(for:club:)` finds only an open deal of the player's present kind, the rule `recordRound` uses; a blank figure typed for a club with no entry saves nothing; a deal opened for a club with no figure entered keeps no figures.
- **On the screen.** "Record my proposal" and "Record their bid" are offered beside a club's problem line too, since a club's bid needs no figures and could not otherwise be recorded; the club's four figure fields appear once a club is named; a loan to move a wage has no playing-time menu in the editor, since such a loan asks none. The deletion questions read "Delete this deal and its rounds from the record?" and "Delete this ask from the record?". Under "Earlier offers" each card's heading and each ask names the player ("Old Boy · Barnet · ended"), since nothing else says whose they are.
- **The plan's calls beyond the design all stood**: a loan gets one counter; "Waiting for Barnet's answer."; no command on the command line; the mark sets the kind; money written in full; a club's wage figures saved by the week; the later money's parts always add up.

## Limits and small faults left

The design's limits (its section 11), as built:

- Every estimate is carried over from an older version or is this tool's own, and the game was patched five days before the design was written.
- The measures show only what the owner tried. If he never asks above the line, the line cannot rise; a higher ask that is accepted is what moves it.
- The lines put clubs of every level together, and a loan turned down for want of wage budget counts as turned down on price when the club's figures were not entered.
- A value typed weeks ago is used as typed. The day beside it is the only warning.
- Whether a club agrees to money beyond its budget is not known, and at this level most proposals will ask for some.
- The contract types FMRTE pastes other than full-time and youth are not interpreted (the captured squad has one written "4"), so a non-contract player, who is reported to bring no fee and to be barred from loans, is not recognised.
- The playing-time levels on FM27's loan screen have not been read; the owner says whether a club's answer is a starting place.
- The loan limits of the owner's league are not read. A club that has used its loans will refuse whatever is asked.

Small faults found by the task reviews and left (the whole-branch review may fix some):

- A deal with several accepted rounds uses the earliest for the counter step.
- A loan to move his wage notes an optional fee to buy him at the sale's ask, which uses a measured sale line when there is one, with no note saying so.
- `.agreed` is returned when any earlier round was accepted, even if later rounds follow it.
- A value with too many digits to hold clears the saved value.
- A club entry whose figures were all blanked again still shows "entered …".
- An ask's "No bid came" and "Delete" sit at the reading width, away from the end of its line.
- The value and budget fields show bare digits with no currency sign.
- One data check is still in a view: whether "To clubs in general" is shown.
- A counter's sentence writes the club's own later money and the difference added as one figure, "later, after 50 league appearances" (accepted in Task 2).

Not checked: the editor sheet, the two deletion questions, the "Measure again from today" question and the menus in a live window, since the pictures draw controls as an inactive window would.
